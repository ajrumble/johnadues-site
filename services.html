Frequently asked questions

A few things you may be wondering.

Do I need to know exactly what my book is about?+
No. You should have meaningful expertise, experiences, or ideas to share, but part of the Blueprint process is identifying the strongest central argument and the reader it can help.
What if I want the complete book right away?+
You can choose Book Blueprint + Book Build from the start for $15,000 total. The Blueprint stage establishes the foundation, and we then develop a complete 30,000–40,000-word manuscript. We'll confirm scope, schedule, and fit before signing an agreement.
What happens if I buy Blueprint first and decide to continue?+
If you proceed to Book Build within 90 days, your $2,500 Blueprint fee is credited toward the $15,000 full engagement, leaving $12,500. We will confirm the next phase in a separate agreement.
How much of my time does Blueprint require?+
Plan on four hours total: approximately 30 minutes for the intake questionnaire, a 75-minute Discovery interview, a 75-minute Architecture interview, and a 60-minute manuscript review. The work takes place over four weeks.
Do you use AI to write books?+
Yes. I use AI tools as part of a structured, interview-driven writing process. Your knowledge, stories, and expertise form the foundation. AI helps organize research, produce initial drafts, and support revision; I direct the work, review and refine the writing, and incorporate your feedback. Because AI contributes to substantive drafting, the manuscript may require AI-content disclosure when published. I provide information about that use to help you navigate publishing requirements. If you intend to pursue traditional publishing, we should discuss that goal before beginning, as a different workflow may be necessary.
Does Book Build include publishing the book?+
No. Book Build delivers a complete manuscript, not cover design, final typesetting, printing, distribution, or publication. We can discuss your intended publishing path and what you'll need next.
How does payment work?+
A little old-fashioned: after we agree on the project, I'll send you an agreement and invoice. Payment is by check. No shopping cart, no checkout countdown, and no surprise card-processing fees. We'll confirm payment timing in the agreement.
Is this right for every kind of nonfiction book?+
The standard process is designed for practical nonfiction built around a person's professional knowledge, leadership experience, business ideas, or expertise. Memoirs, heavily researched narrative nonfiction, and projects intended for traditional publishers may require a different scope.
